💡 Travel Tips

Reach Ganga Aarti at least 45–60 minutes early for a good viewing spot.
Dress modestly while visiting temples.
Carry cash for local street food and boat rides.
Drink bottled water only.
Use e-rickshaws or walk inside old Banaras lanes.
